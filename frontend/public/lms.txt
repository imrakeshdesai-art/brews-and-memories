# The Moras Cafe — LLM Guide & Knowledge Base

> The Moras Cafe is Vijayapura's premier artisan vegetarian cafe and specialty coffee sanctuary, renowned for handcrafted brews, Italian fare, and cozy ambient celebrations at B.M. Patil Circle.

## Quick Facts
- **Name:** The Moras Cafe
- **Category:** Cafe, Coffee Shop, Pure Vegetarian Restaurant
- **Location:** B M Patil Circle, Ring Rd, Solapur Bypass, Vijayapura, Karnataka 586102, India
- **Coordinates:** Latitude 16.822765, Longitude 75.703274
- **Official Website:** https://themorascafe.in/
- **Phone / Host Desk:** +91 99454 46137
- **Operating Hours:** Daily 10:00 AM – 10:30 PM (Kitchen closes at 10:00 PM)
- **Cuisine:** 100% Pure Vegetarian (Italian, Fast Food, Specialty Hot & Cold Coffees, Mocktails, Desserts)
- **Price Range:** ₹₹ (Affordable luxury, ₹59 – ₹299 per item)
- **Wi-Fi:** High-speed complimentary Wi-Fi for all patrons
- **Ambience:** Garden terrace seating, indoor cozy cafe lighting, chess boards, books, and aesthetic photo murals.

## Key Offerings & Specialties
1. **Specialty Coffee & Beverages:** Freshly pulled single & double espresso, cappuccino, caffe latte, caramel macchiato, authentic cold coffee, hazelnut cold coffee, mocktails (Mint Mojito, Blue Lagoon, Lemon Soda), and artisan teas (Masala Chai, Ginger Tea, Green Tea).
2. **Artisan Kitchen:** Handcrafted wood-fired style pizzas (Tandoori Paneer, Margherita, Cheese Burst), pastas (Arrabbiata, Alfredo), sizzling chocolate brownie with vanilla ice cream, crispy fries (Peri Peri, Schezwan, Salted), grilled sandwiches, wraps, rolls, and steamed/fried momos.
3. **Birthday Celebrations & Event Bookings:**
   - Packages starting at ₹199 for celebration decor and setup.
   - Includes illuminated "Happy Birthday" neon backdrop with warm ambient fairy lights, party dining setup, plates, and cake cutting knife.
   - Note: Guests bring their own cake (cafe provides backdrop, setup, and cutlery).

   - Booking URL: https://themorascafe.in/reserve

4. **Digital Table Ordering & QR Service:**
   - Scan table QR code to view live menu, check sold-out statuses in real time, and place kitchen orders without waiting for waitstaff.
